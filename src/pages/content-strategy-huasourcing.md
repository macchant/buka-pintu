## 4. Blog Post Format Template

Every post follows this structure. Consistent format = faster writing + better SEO.

### Template Sections (in order)

```
1. FEATURED IMAGE
   — Large, relevant, branded
   — Alt text: [Post Title] | Huasourcing

2. TITLE (H1)
   — Include primary keyword
   — Format: "[Primary Keyword]: [Specific Benefit]"
   — Example: "Incoterms 2026 Explained: Stop Losing Margins to Hidden Import Fees"

3. INTRO (150-200 words)
   — Hook with a problem the reader has
   — State what this post will solve
   — One sentence on why you're qualified to write it

4. TABLE OF CONTENTS
   — Auto-generated by Rank Math or Yoast
   — H2 headings become anchor links

5. BODY CONTENT
   — H2 for each major section
   — H3 for sub-sections
   — Every H2 = one potential ad placement zone
   — Include at least 2 internal links per 1,000 words
   — Include at least 1 image with alt text per 500 words

6. PULL QUOTE or CALLOUT BOX
   — Key stat or takeaway, highlighted
   — Good visual break for readers

7. FAQ SECTION (at least 5 questions)
   — Use H2 "Frequently Asked Questions"
   — Schema markup: FAQPage (critical for featured snippets)
   — Each question ends with a direct, specific answer

8. CONCLUSION (200-300 words)
   — Summarize 3 key takeaways
   — Natural CTA: "Want us to handle your next import? Get a sourcing plan."
   — Link to related posts

9. INTERNAL LINKS
   — Minimum 3 links to other blog posts
   — Minimum 1 link to a service page
```

## 5. AdSense Placement Strategy

Place ads strategically — they must not interrupt the reading experience.

### Ad Placement Rules
- **Leaderboard ad** — After the intro paragraph, before the first H2
- **In-article ad** — After every 3rd H2 section (not after H3s)
- **Rectangle ad** — Sidebar (if blog has a sidebar) or inside a break box
- **Never** — Within the first paragraph, inside a list, immediately after a heading, or before the TOC

### Ad Density
- Maximum 3 ads per 1,000 words of body content
- At 1,500 words = max 4 ads
- At 3,000 words = max 8 ads
- Better to have 2-3 well-placed ads than 8 poorly-placed ones

### Disable Ads On
- `/contact` — Lead page
- `/quote` or any form page
- Homepage — your hero CTA is more valuable than an ad

## 6. Internal Linking Strategy

Internal links are free SEO and keep users on-site (more impressions).

### Link Every Post To:
- At least 1 related Tier 1 cornerstone post
- At least 2 Tier 2 supporting posts
- At least 1 service page

### Add Links In:
- Body text (naturally, within context)
- FAQ section (within each answer)
- Conclusion (related posts list)
- Within 30 days of publishing: add links TO the new post from 2-3 existing posts

### Anchor Text Rules
- Use descriptive anchor text: "learn about incoterms" not "click here"
- Exact match keyword anchors are fine in body content
- Never use "read more" or "here" as anchor text

## 7. Content Quality Standards

### Every Post Must Have
- [ ] Primary keyword in H1 title
- [ ] Primary keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] At least 1 H2 per 400 words
- [ ] At least 1 image with descriptive alt text
- [ ] Internal links (minimum 3)
- [ ] External link to a credible source (gov site, industry authority)
- [ ] FAQ section with FAQPage schema
- [ ] A clear conclusion with CTA
- [ ] Featured image (1200×630px minimum, WebP format)

### Every Post Should Avoid
- Thin content under 800 words (Google flags this)
- Keyword stuffing (natural density only)
- Duplicate content from your own site
- Outdated statistics without dates
- No-following all external links (Google notices this)

## 8. Content Promotion

Publishing is only half the work. Promote every post.

### Week 1 After Publishing
1. Submit to Google Search Console (URL Inspection → Request Indexing)
2. Share on LinkedIn (your professional network = your target audience)
3. Share in relevant Facebook groups (import/export/business startup groups)
4. Link from the existing related posts that already rank

### Ongoing
- Refresh cornerstone posts every 6 months (update stats, add new sections)
- Delete or archive posts with 0 traffic after 6 months
- Monitor Search Console for keywords the post ranks for but doesn't mention
- Add a "Related Posts" section to every new post (use a plugin like "Related Posts for WordPress")
